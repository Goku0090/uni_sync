Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about UniSinq.

To create your student profile:

  1. Sign up for an account
  2. Complete your profile setup with your college, interests, and skills
  3. Add your professional links (GitHub, LinkedIn, etc.)
  4. Upload a profile photo

A complete profile helps you get better collaborator recommendations and increases your visibility in the community.

Finding collaborators is easy:

  1. Go to the "Find Collaborators" page
  2. Use filters to search by skills, college, or location
  3. Browse suggested collaborators based on your interests
  4. Send connection requests to potential collaborators
  5. Once connected, you can discuss projects via direct messages

To post a project:

  1. Click "Post Project" from your dashboard
  2. Fill in project details: title, description, technologies, category
  3. Specify what type of collaborators you're looking for
  4. Add a timeline and collaboration requirements
  5. Include your GitHub repository link if available

Well-written project posts attract more qualified collaborators.

Our AI-powered matching system considers:

  • Skills Match (40%): How well your skills align with project requirements
  • Interests Match (25%): Shared interests and project preferences
  • Availability (15%): Time commitment compatibility
  • Role Match (10%): Preferred roles and project needs
  • Location (10%): Geographical preferences

Higher match scores indicate better compatibility for collaboration.

Project team management features:

  • Create teams for your projects
  • Invite collaborators via team invitations
  • Assign roles (Owner, Contributor, Viewer)
  • Create and assign tasks with deadlines
  • Set project milestones and track progress
  • Use team chat for communication

All core features including project posting, collaborator matching, messaging, and team management are available at no cost.
