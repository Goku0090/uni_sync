Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with UniSinq.

Is UniSinq free to use?

Yes! UniSinq offers a free tier with all essential features. We also have premium plans for teams that need advanced collaboration tools and priority support.

Who can join UniSinq?

UniSinq is designed for students, recent graduates, and young professionals. We welcome anyone passionate about building innovative projects and learning new technologies.

How does the matching algorithm work?

Our smart matching considers your skills, interests, project preferences, and collaboration style to connect you with the most compatible teammates and project opportunities.

Can I work on multiple projects at once?

Absolutely! Many students juggle multiple projects simultaneously. Our platform supports multiple project memberships and helps you manage your time effectively across different teams.

What if I don't have technical skills?

No problem! UniSinq is for all types of creators. Whether you're a designer, marketer, business developer, or have domain expertise, you'll find projects that need your unique skills.

How do I get started?

Simply sign up, complete your profile, and start exploring projects. You can join existing projects or post your own idea to find collaborators. It's that easy!
