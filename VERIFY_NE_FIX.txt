================================================================================
                           QUICK REFERENCE
================================================================================

The Problem:
  - Django ORM doesn't support __ne (not equal) operator
  - Tried to use: message__sender__ne=user
  - Result: FieldError

The Solution:
  - Use exclude() instead
  - Changed: .filter(..., field__ne=value)
  - To: .filter(...).exclude(field=value)

Why It Works:
  - exclude() removes items from results
  - Achieves "not equal" with proper Django syntax
  - Works with all field types including ForeignKey
